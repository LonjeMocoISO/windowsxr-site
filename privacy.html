Windows XR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Windows XR brings selected Windows applications into panels on Meta Quest. This policy explains the information used by the Quest app, Windows XR Connect on your PC, and the connection website at windowsxr.com.

Privacy and support contact: andersiniedo@gmail.com.

How the connection works

The Windows applications run on your PC. Windows XR Connect discovers the available applications and sends the selected windows to the paired Quest. Your actions in those panels are sent back to the PC.

The current wireless connection uses your local network, such as a PC hotspot. Pairing uses a six-digit code. The connection website communicates with the companion on your own PC to help you authorize the connection and obtain that code. A Windows XR account is not required.

This local connection is separate from the internet services used by the website, downloads, Meta Quest, and your Windows applications. For example, a browser, cloud document or messaging app you open through Windows XR can still send information to its own provider.

Information used to provide Windows XR

  • Application and window information. Application names, icons, launch information and window metadata are used to build Home, open the application you choose, fit its window and maintain the panel.
  • Selected window content and input. Images of the windows you choose are transmitted to your Quest. Clicks, scrolling, text selection, text you enter, and clipboard commands you choose are sent to the PC to operate those applications. The content can include personal or sensitive information displayed or entered in the application.
  • Connection and workspace information. Local network addresses, connection settings, session credentials and panel/task descriptors are used for pairing, authentication and safe reconnection. Some of this information is stored privately on your devices so the app can check an existing connection when reopened.
  • Local diagnostics. The app and companion keep technical records such as status and error categories, timing measurements, event counts and connection or capture metadata. These support local troubleshooting and can be used for support if you choose to share them. Application/window information and local session records may also be present in the companion's private data folders.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, pairing codes, session credentials or sensitive window content.

Where information goes

For the current local-network streaming feature, the selected-window stream and control messages travel between your PC and Quest through Windows XR Connect. The current app and companion do not implement a Windows XR cloud relay for that stream.

The current client and companion do not automatically submit their local diagnostic files to a Windows XR support server. You can choose whether to share relevant information when asking for help.

The public website and downloadable releases use GitHub and GitHub Pages. Visiting the site or downloading a file sends a normal internet request to the relevant provider. Hosting, download, browser and network infrastructure providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, request times and browser or device details, under their own policies.

Meta provides the Quest operating system and may provide app distribution, system input and other platform services. Your use of those services is also governed by Meta's policies. Windows, your browser, email services and the Windows applications you use have their own data practices. This policy does not replace their policies or describe all information those providers may handle.

The current Windows XR Android client does not include an advertising or third-party analytics SDK. The public connection website uses Umami Cloud for the limited website analytics described below. This website measurement is separate from the Quest app, Windows companion and Windows application content.

Website analytics

We use Umami Cloud to understand visits to windowsxr.com, approximate visitor counts and countries, and how often visitors activate the APK and Windows XR Connect download buttons. A button activation does not confirm that a file finished downloading or was installed. GitHub provides separate download counters for the hosted files.

Our website sends a fixed page path and title, browser language, screen dimensions and, for a download button, the event name download_apk or download_connect. It does not send form contents, pairing codes, PC network addresses, session credentials, window content, URL query strings, URL fragments or referring-page URLs to Umami. We use our own limited collection code; we do not load a third-party analytics script into the connection page or enable session replay.

Like other internet requests, an analytics request exposes your public IP address and browser user-agent to the receiving service. Umami processes request information to derive approximate location and visitor/browser statistics under its privacy policy. Countries can be affected by VPNs and network routing, and visitor counts are estimates rather than verified people.

This measurement does not use analytics cookies or save an analytics identifier in your browser. The analytics project uses Umami's US data region. Our current Umami Hobby plan retains reporting data for six months. Umami and its infrastructure providers may retain operational information under their own policies.

You can turn website analytics off for this browser below. The website stores only that preference in local storage. We also skip measurement when the browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Blocking analytics or turning it off does not prevent downloads, pairing or use of Windows XR.

Website analytics respects your browser privacy choices. This control requires JavaScript.

Local storage and retention

The Quest app uses app-private storage for connection information and local diagnostics. Windows XR Connect stores its own runtime data and diagnostics in your Windows user profile. Private storage is not a promise that every file is encrypted at rest.

There is no single fixed retention period for all local data. Settings, cached information and diagnostics can remain until they are replaced, cleared or removed. Disconnecting a session stops that connection; it does not automatically erase every stored record.

Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox while it is needed to respond to the request or keep a record of the issue. Hosting, distribution and email providers control retention in their own systems.

Your choices

  • Choose which PC to pair and which application windows to open. Pair only with devices and people you trust.
  • Use Disconnect on the connection page or quit Windows XR Connect to end the PC connection. Closing only the website does not stop an already active Quest session.
  • Control the website's local-network permission through your browser. A permitted own-page presence check helps Connect reuse the setup page; that check alone does not start capture or authorize control of Windows applications.
  • Use your Quest's app-storage controls or uninstall the app to remove its device-local data. Uninstalling the Windows companion may leave runtime data in the Windows user profile; removing that data is a separate step.
  • Contact us for help identifying Windows XR data or making a privacy request. Because much of the information stays on your devices, we normally cannot access or remove it remotely. Requests concerning another provider's systems should also be directed to that provider.

Permissions and security

The current Quest app requests internet/network access for its connection. It does not request camera, microphone or scene-data permissions. Horizon OS supplies the system window presentation and interaction used by the app.

The wireless pairing and connection use code-based authentication and TLS certificate checks. Access to your PC, Quest or stored session information can expose application content or control. Keep your devices secure and do not share connection credentials. No security measure can guarantee complete protection in every situation.

Intended audience

Windows XR is intended for people aged 13 and older.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Windows XR changes. The updated policy will identify its revision date. Review it when choosing to use a new version or a new feature.
