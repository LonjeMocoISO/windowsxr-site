Local network
  1. 01

    Connect your Quest to your PC’s hotspot.

    Turn on Mobile hotspot in Windows, then choose that network in your Quest's Wi-Fi settings. Keep the hotspot on throughout your session.

  2. 02

    Download Windows XR Connect.

    Select Download Windows XR Connect to save the installer on the PC whose apps you want to use.

  3. 03

    Install Windows XR Connect, then open it from Start.

    Run the downloaded installer, then open Windows XR Connect from the Windows Start menu. Keep it running while you use your headset.

  4. 04

    Select Get my code here. If your browser asks, allow access to the local network.

    Choose your PC's hotspot network and select Allow connection to display your six-digit code on this page.

  5. 05

    Enter the six-digit code in the Windows XR app on Quest, before the splash.

    Open Windows XR in your headset, enter the code shown here, then select Connect or press Enter. After pairing, the splash plays and your app library opens.

Headset setup
  1. 01

    Download the APK on your PC or headset.

    On your PC, use the download button. Or visit www.windowsxr.com in your headset browser and download the APK there.

    1A If you downloaded it on your PC, transfer the APK to your headset.

  2. 02

    Install and open with Package Installer

    Open the downloaded APK with Package Installer, then select Install.

  3. 03

    Open Windows XR on your headset.

    Once installed, open Windows XR from your headset's Unknown Sources library.

  4. 04

    Continue with the PC setup.

    Install Windows XR Connect on your PC, get your six-digit code and enter it in Windows XR on your headset.

Downloading or transferring the APK does not install it. If APK installation is not available on your headset, use Meta VR Developer Hub on your PC.

Keep your Quest and PC on the same hotspot when pairing Windows XR.
